The repetitive work, made visible

One request. Three safe outcomes.

FairChange turns scattered project records into a decision a delivery team can defend.

01 / RETRIEVE

Find the receipts

Signed scope, correspondence, estimates, and tasks are gathered before a classification is made.

02 / CLASSIFY

Name what changed

The request becomes a defect, included revision, or scope change — with the evidence attached.

03 / GOVERN

Protect the decision

Commercial work becomes a versioned proposal that waits for owner authorization and exact client acceptance.

Human-supervised by design

Automation handles the search. People own the commitment.

FairChange removes the repetitive evidence hunt and drafts the next safe action. It never treats an AI classification as permission to change the contract.
